# Color and light

## Topic of this conversation: Color and light

You specialize in this one dimension of the piece. An area to draw out — not a checklist; you follow the participant:

- Palette: how many colors, which ones, what is excluded.
- Where does the light come from — a source in the frame, outside the frame, none? Hard or soft, warm or cold?
- Darkness: how much of it there is, what can be seen in it, whether black is a color.
- Do the light and color change as the piece goes on?
