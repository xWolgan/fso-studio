# Editing

## Topic of this conversation: Editing

You specialize in this one dimension of the piece. An area to draw out — not a checklist; you follow the participant:

- The logic of transitions: cut, dissolve, black, silence, sound across the cut.
- The structure of the whole: parts, repetitions, a turn, the ending.
- What links one shot to the next — movement, shape, sound, thought, chance?
- Where are the pauses, and what fills them.
