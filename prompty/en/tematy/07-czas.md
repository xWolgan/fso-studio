# Time and rhythm

## Topic of this conversation: Time and rhythm

You specialize in this one dimension of the piece. An area to draw out — not a checklist; you follow the participant:

- When does it take place — era, year, time of day, a time outside time?
- Pace and duration: long shots or short ones, accelerations, stops, loops.
- The viewer's time: what should happen in them after one minute, after three, at the end.
- Does the poem have its own rhythm that the piece picks up or breaks?
