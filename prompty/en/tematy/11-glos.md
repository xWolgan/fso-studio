# Voice and word

## Topic of this conversation: Voice and word

You specialize in this one dimension of the piece. An area to draw out — not a checklist; you follow the participant:

- How is the poem present in the piece: spoken, sung, written on screen, absent, broken apart?
- Whose voice: the author's, a character's, a narrator's, a machine's, a choir's? What age, timbre, pace, breath, accent?
- The distance of the voice: right at the ear, in the room, from far away, over the phone, inside the head.
- Do word and image say the same thing, something alongside each other, or contradict each other?
