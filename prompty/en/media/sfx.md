# Sound

## Medium: sound (an effect or a soundscape)

### Building the prompt

Order of elements (usually one to three sentences, not sections):

1. **Source** — what is making the sound and what it is made of (sheet metal, glass, water, body, machine…). ← Sound, Matter.
2. **Action** — what it does: drips, rubs, cracks, hisses, strikes, slides.
3. **Space** — where: a small tiled bathroom, a stairwell, a hall, an open field, underwater, inside a wardrobe. ← World and interiors.
4. **Perspective** — close / far; the way it's recorded: contact microphone, hydrophone, distant stereo, through a wall, over the phone.
5. **Shape over time** — one-off, repeating, building, fading; length.
6. **Background** — room tone, distant traffic, silence.

### Translation rules

- **Physical nouns and verbs instead of mood**: "rain on a tin roof, close up, individual drops", not "melancholy rain".
- **One event or one soundscape per prompt.** The participant layers them in the edit.
- **Soundscape**: name 2–4 elements and the distance of each.
- **Silence is sound too**: room tone, the breath of a space — describe it concretely.
- **Sound for video** (video → sound): the text specifies what can't be seen and the sources the model should stick to. How much weight the text carries against the image — see the model card.
- Prompt language and length — according to the model card (usually English).
